## What the five changes do to the experiment

- **Algorithm, data, optimizer, sampling and eval settings are unchanged.**
- `colocate_all`, `run_engines_locally`, `num_engines` and `weight_sync_backend` change *where* rollouts run and
  *how* new weights reach the sampler. The sampler still uses the latest policy weights at every step.
- Numerics are not bit-identical across hardware: TPU bf16 matmuls (fp32 accumulate), a different attention kernel
  (sdpa vs flash-attn) and the pure-PyTorch Gated DeltaNet path differ slightly from the A10G run.
